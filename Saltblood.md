## Overview

Saltbloods are humans spliced with fish genetics to settle ocean worlds where dry land is scarce, drowned, or poisoned. They have gills along the neck and ribs, a second inner eyelid, and skin that ranges from fine scales to smooth shark-like denticles. Most can pass for baseline humans from a distance, until they take off their shirt or go underwater and stay there.

## Biology

A Saltblood breathes through lungs and gills alike, switching between them without thinking. A lateral line, a row of pressure-sensitive pores running from jaw to hip, lets them feel movement in the water around them, even in total darkness.

The cost is dryness. Saltblood skin and gills must be kept wet. A day or two away from water leaves them cracked, sore, and short of breath; longer than that and they begin to fail.

Saltbloods fall into broad lineages depending on the fish whose genes they carry: sleek, powerful **Selachian** (shark) stock; flat, graceful **Batoid** (ray) stock; long, sinuous **Anguilline** (eel) stock; bright, social **Reef** stock; and pale, wide-eyed **Abyssal** stock bred for the deep.

## Society

Saltblood communities are built along coastlines, on floating platforms, and in sealed habitats on the sea floor. Wealth is counted in boats, nets, and dive rights. Many Saltbloods distrust "drylanders" who see the ocean only as something to cross, and drylanders in turn often treat Saltbloods as useful hired hands for anything wet and dangerous.

Off-world, Saltbloods find steady work as divers, salvagers, hydroponics crew, and sailors, and are known to pay extra for quarters with a tank or a proper bath.

## Presence on Prometheus

Small Saltblood fishing towns cling to the coasts and islands, often trading fish and salvage for goods from inland settlements. A few sealed sea-floor habitats are rumoured to exist, though few drylanders have seen one.

## Saltblood Traveller Traits

### Aquatic Lineage

Choose one lineage:

- **Selachian** (shark): +1 **STR** and +1 **END**.
- **Batoid** (ray, skate): +1 **DEX** and +1 **INT**.
- **Anguilline** (eel, lamprey): +1 **DEX** and +1 **END**.
- **Reef** (reef fish, seahorse, pufferfish): +1 **DEX** and +1 **SOC**.
- **Abyssal** (anglerfish, deep-sea fish): +1 **INT** and +1 **END**.

> **Custom Lineage Rules**
> 
> 1. A custom lineage must be based on a **fish**. Sharks and rays count as fish.
> 2. Marine mammals (whales, dolphins, seals), cephalopods, and crustaceans are **not** Saltblood lineages. Land animals belong to the Yttakin or Herdkin.
> 3. The bonuses must total **2**, and each improves the actual characteristic, not its DM.

### Amphibious

Saltbloods can breathe both air and water. They swim at their full Movement, and ignore the DM penalties for acting underwater.

They can safely dive to **100 metres** without equipment. **Abyssal** Saltbloods can safely dive to **1,000 metres**.

### Lateral Line

While submerged, a Saltblood senses every moving creature within **20 metres**, even in total darkness, and cannot be surprised by them.

### Child of the Sea

Gain **+1 Seafarer** (any speciality) or **+1 Athletics**.

### Desiccation

A Saltblood must spend at least **1 hour fully submerged** in water each day. Salt or fresh water both work; a full bathtub is enough.

For each day without it, the Saltblood takes a cumulative **DM-1** to all checks (to a maximum of **DM-3**). After the third day, it also loses **1 END** per further day. One full hour of immersion removes all of these penalties, and lost END recovers as normal.

### Natural Weapon (Optional)

A Saltblood may take its lineage's natural weapon at the cost of **-1 SOC**.

- **Selachian, Jaws:** a bite attack that deals **2D** damage.
- **Batoid, Barbed Tail:** a tail-stinger attack that deals **2D** damage.
- **Anguilline, Electric Organ:** a touch attack that deals **2D** damage and has the **Stun** trait. Underwater, it affects every creature in contact with the same body of water within 2 metres.
- **Reef, Venomous Spines:** anyone who grapples the Saltblood or hits it with an unarmed attack takes **1D** damage.
- **Abyssal, Lure:** a bioluminescent organ that lights a **5 metre** radius at will. The Saltblood ignores DM penalties from dim light.
