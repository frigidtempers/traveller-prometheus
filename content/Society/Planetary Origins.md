The world you were raised on shapes your outlook as much as your xenotype or career. From the comfort of glitterworld utopias to the harsh frontier of the Rim, every civilization leaves its mark upon those who call it home.

During character creation, choose the planetary origin that best represents where your Traveller spent their formative years. Each origin grants a choice between two unique traits that reflect the skills, values, and hardships common to its people.

---

## Glitterworld

*"Every dystopia is a utopia for someone else."*

**Tech Level:** 15+

The pinnacle of human civilization, glitterworlds are societies of abundance built upon automation, genetic engineering, and advanced medicine. Disease is virtually unknown, labor is largely automated, and comfort is taken for granted. Beneath their polished exterior, however, many remain dominated by corporate dynasties, hereditary nobility, or crushing debt.

### Choose One Origin Trait

#### Utopian Elegance

Gain **+1 SOC**.

If aligned with the **Shattered Imperium**, your Traveller may qualify for **Nobility** during character creation.

#### Captured by Muses

Gain **Art (Any)** at level 1.

---

## Urbworld

*"Well-lit streets discourage sin, but don't overdo it."*

**Tech Level:** 10

Immense city-planets whose populations long ago outpaced their infrastructure. Endless megacities stretch from horizon to horizon, where pollution, overcrowding, and crime are facts of everyday life. Survival often depends upon quick instincts and harder fists.

### Choose One Origin Trait

#### Hard Knocks

Gain **+1 Streetwise** and **+1 Carouse**.

#### Urbworld Hospitality

Choose one:

- **+1 STR** and **+1 Melee**, or
- **+1 DEX** and **+1 Gun Combat**

---

## Midworld

*"Something old, something new."*

**Tech Level:** 7

Midworlds have mastered atmospheric flight and global industry but have yet to achieve widespread interstellar travel. Many resemble Old Earth during the early twenty-first century, balancing modern technology with lingering national identities and cultural traditions.

### Choose One Origin Trait

#### Hunger for Knowledge

Gain **+1 INT** and **+1 EDU**.

#### Endless Possibility

Gain **Jack of All Trades at Level 1**.

---

## Medieval World

*"Once an abbot, always an abbot."*

**Tech Level:** 2

These worlds remain locked in feudal societies dominated by kingdoms, empires, or religious orders. While technologically primitive, generations of hardship have produced resilient people with a deep respect for tradition and martial prowess.

### Choose One Origin Trait

#### Chivalry

Gain **+1 SOC** and **+1 Diplomat**.

#### Strong but Unknowing

Gain **+2 STR** and **+2 END**.

You may only use **Archaic Weapons** unless trained with more advanced weaponry.

---

## Indworld

*"The Industrial Revolution and its consequences have been a disaster for the human race."*

**Tech Level:** 6

Factory cities, sprawling foundries, and endless assembly lines define the industrial worlds. Their economies revolve around manufacturing, resource extraction, and heavy industry, producing generations of skilled workers accustomed to demanding labor.

### Choose One Origin Trait

#### Losing Your Chains

Gain **+1 STR** and **+1 END**.

#### An Injury to One

Gain **+1 Mechanic** and **+1 Profession**.

---

## Rimworld

*"Living on the edge."*

**Tech Level:** Varies

Rimworlds exist beyond the reach of centralized authority. Settlements are isolated, governments are weak or nonexistent, and technology ranges from medieval villages to glittertech ruins. Ancient dangers, crashed starships, and wandering travellers ensure that no two Rimworlds are ever alike.

### Choose One Origin Trait

#### Frontier Spirit

Gain **+1 Survival** and **+1 Recon**.

#### Self-Reliant

Gain **+1 Gun Combat** and **+1 Mechanic**.
