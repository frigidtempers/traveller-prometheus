
Reputation is **character-specific**. If your character dies, all of their reputations reset to their starting values.

### Categories and Factions

Every group on Prometheus is an individual **faction**, and every faction belongs to a **category**.

- You track reputation with each **faction** separately.
- A faction's **category** decides which benefits you unlock with it, and how its reputation spreads to others of its kind.

| Category             | Factions                                                                                                |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Civil Outlanders** | Oakbridge, Ravenfort, The Desert Republic                                                               |
| **Rough Outlanders** | Eenti's Gang, Lowmarket, Sanguophage Society, The Mob                                                   |
| **Corporate Houses** | House Amora, House Belmar (including the Mek-Kaniks), House Gol, House Helix, House Xiaomu, Gas Monkeys |
| **Ideoligions**      | Imperial Inquisition, Order of Martyrs, Procession of the Sacred Cog                                    |
| **Mercenaries**      | Misriah PMC                                                                                             |

| **Shattered Empire** | Shattered Empire (Stellarch Cassian Aurelios)                                                           |
| **Tribals**          | Impid Tribe, Nomads                                                                                     |

### The Reputation Scale

Reputation runs from **-10 to +10**.

|Rep|Standing|Social DM|
|---|---|---|
|+9 to +10|Honoured|+3|
|+6 to +8|Trusted|+2|
|+3 to +5|Known|+1|
|-2 to +2|Neutral|+0|
|-3 to -5|Distrusted|-1|
|-6 to -8|Hated|-2|
|-9 to -10|Enemy|-3|

The **Social DM** applies to Persuade, Diplomat, Broker, Carouse, Streetwise and similar checks made with members of that faction.

**Starting reputation:** new characters start at **+1** with most factions and at **-2** with the **Raiders**.

### Gaining and Losing Reputation

|Action|Change|
|---|---|
|Complete a bounty or quest for the faction|+1|
|Complete a major quest, or save the faction from a serious threat|+2|
|Rescue or return a captured member|+1|
|Give a gift worth Cr5,000 or more (once per faction per month)|+1|
|Fail or abandon a contract for the faction|-1|
|Steal from, cheat or publicly insult the faction|-1|
|Recruit one of the faction's NPCs to your own settlement|-1|
|Attack or injure a member|-2|
|Kill a member, or raid the faction|-3|

- **Climbing gets harder.** Gifts can't raise reputation above **+5**. Going beyond that takes quests and deeds.
- **Witnesses matter.** Reputation only changes if the faction finds out.
- **Shared deeds.** Every character who took part gets the change.

### How Reputation Spreads

Each category handles reputation between its factions in one of two ways.

- **Kindred:** the factions talk to each other. A faction you haven't dealt with treats you as having **half your best reputation** in the category (round down). If your worst reputation in the category is **-6 or lower**, they use **half of that** instead.
- **Rivals:** the factions compete, and reputation doesn't spread. When you gain reputation with a faction by acting **against** one of its rivals, you lose the same amount with that rival.

|Category|Type|
|---|---|
|Civil Outlanders|Kindred|
|Merchants|Kindred|
|Tribals|Kindred|
|Rough Outlanders|Rivals|
|Corporate Houses|Rivals|
|Ideoligions|Rivals|
|Mercenaries|Rivals|
|Pirates|Rivals|
|Shattered Empire|Rivals (between the Stellarch and noble houses)|

### Opposed Factions

Some groups are natural enemies. When you gain reputation with a faction through an act **against** a group it opposes, you lose the same amount with the group you acted against.

|Faction or category|Opposed to|
|---|---|
|Pirates|Civil Outlanders, Corporate Houses, Merchants, Shattered Empire|
|Shattered Empire|Pirates, Sanguophage Society|
|Imperial Inquisition|Sanguophage Society, Procession of the Sacred Cog|
|Tribals|Whoever is encroaching on their territory|

**Imperial law:** characters the Empire considers proscribed (pirates, Awakened Androids, Sanguophages) can't rise above **+3** with any Shattered Empire faction or the Imperial Inquisition unless a noble patron vouches for them.

### Category Benefits and Consequences

You get these with each faction separately, based on your reputation with **that** faction. Higher tiers include the lower ones.

### Civil Outlanders

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|10% discount on Standard of Living costs while living in that settlement.|
|+6|You can recruit that settlement's NPCs for your own settlement without losing reputation.|
|+9|That settlement's NPCs become Allies, with the appropriate benefits.|
|-3|Standard of Living costs 10% more there, and the militia watches you.|
|-6|You are barred from the settlement.|
|-9|The settlement's militia attacks you on sight.|

### Rough Outlanders

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|Access to that faction's territory and vendors.|
|+6|A safe house: while you lie low with the faction, bounty hunters and the law can't find you.|
|+9|The faction sends muscle to help defend your settlement when it is raided.|
|-3|Its vendors charge you 25% more.|
|-6|You are jumped whenever you enter its territory.|
|-9|The faction puts a contract out on you.|

### Corporate Houses

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|5% discount with that House's vendors.|
|+6|Its vendors offer you a "Secret Supply" of items.|
|+9|Its vendors will buy any item you sell at 80% of its price.|
|-3|Its vendors charge you 10% more.|
|-6|Its vendors refuse to deal with you.|
|-9|The House posts a bounty on you.|

**Subsidiaries:** a House's subsidiaries share its reputation. The **Mek-Kaniks**, Mad Mek's clone collective and their chain of workshops, belong to **House Belmar**, so your House Belmar reputation applies at every Mek-Kanik shop. Corporate security guards answer to whichever House employs them.

**Gas Monkeys** use their own list instead of the Corporate House benefits:

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|10% discount on vehicle fuel while travelling.|
|+6|The Gas Monkeys will help you source any ground vehicle.|
|+9|Gas Monkey technicians will help with Gravship construction.|
|-3|Fuel costs you 25% more.|
|-6|The Gas Monkeys refuse to sell to you or repair your vehicles.|
|-9|You are blacklisted, and your vehicles are fair game for "salvage".|

### Ideoligions

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|You are welcome at the faith's shrines: free basic lodging and healing.|
|+6|You may take part in the faith's rituals. Once per month, gain a Boon on one check that serves the faith's values.|
|+9|A devoted follower joins your settlement, or the faith entrusts you with one of its relics.|
|-3|The faithful shun you and refuse you shelter.|
|-6|You are declared a heretic: followers refuse all dealings with you.|
|-9|Zealots hunt you.|

### Mercenaries

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|You can hire the company and buy from its quartermaster.|
|+6|20% discount on contracts, and the company will garrison your settlement for a fee.|
|+9|Once per month, the company answers a call for help at no charge.|
|-3|Its rates for you are 25% higher.|
|-6|The company refuses your contracts and accepts contracts against you.|
|-9|The company hunts you as a matter of reputation.|

### Merchants

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|5% discount with caravans and orbital traders.|
|+6|You can place special orders: a caravan brings the item on its next visit.|
|+9|Traders buy your goods at 80% of their price and extend you credit.|
|-3|Traders charge you 10% more.|
|-6|Caravans stop visiting your settlement.|
|-9|You are blacklisted: no merchant will trade with you.|

### Pirates

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|Access to that band's vendors.|
|+6|20% discount with that band's vendors.|
|+9|That band will not raid your settlements, whatever the Security score.|
|-3|That band raids your settlements more often.|
|-6|The band puts a price on your head.|
|-9|The band's captain leads raids against you personally, and its members attack on sight.|

### Shattered Empire

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|Access to Imperial vendors.|
|+6|Gain a royal title, granting a bonus to SOC.|
|+9|Gain either an **Orbital Transmitter**, which lets you call Imperial backup by drop pod during combat, or a **Psylink Neuroformer**.|
|-3|You are searched at every Imperial checkpoint (DM-2 to get anything past them).|
|-6|A warrant is issued: you are arrested on entering Imperial settlements.|
|-9|You are declared proscribed: shot on sight, and Imperial bounty hunters are sent after you.|

### Tribals

|Rep|Effect|
|---|---|
|+3|Access to that tribe's vendors and settlements.|
|+6|Tribals accompany you through their territory, removing Difficult Terrain penalties while travelling.|
|+9|The tribe allows you to access an Anima Tree.|
|-3|You are barred from the tribe's settlements.|
|-6|The tribe ambushes you when you cross its territory.|
|-9|War parties raid your settlement.|
