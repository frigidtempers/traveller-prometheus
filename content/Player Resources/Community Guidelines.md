1. **Be Respectful:** Treat all members of the community with courtesy and respect. Differences in opinion and in-character conflict are acceptable, but personal attacks and harassment are not.
    
2. **Keep Metagaming to a Minimum:** Keep player knowledge and character knowledge separate whenever reasonably possible. Make decisions based on what your character would know and understand.
    
3. **Use the Appropriate Channels:** Keep conversations and content within the channels intended for them. This helps keep the server organized and makes information easier for everyone to find.
    
4. **No Spamming:** Avoid excessive messages, repeated content, unnecessary mentions, or other behavior that disrupts normal conversation.
    
5. **No Sharing Personal Information:** Do not share personal information about yourself or another member without their consent. This includes names, addresses, phone numbers, and other identifying information.
    
6. **Respect Everyone's Privacy:** Do not share private messages, screenshots, or conversations outside of their intended audience without the consent of everyone involved.
    
7. **Don't Be a Freak:** While this is an 18+ community, NSFW material such as erotic roleplay, pornographic images, or similar content is strictly prohibited. Romantic relationships and intimacy between player characters are permitted, but sexual encounters should **fade to black**. Keep explicit sexual content out of the server.
    
8. **Have Fun:** This is a community built around a game. Enjoy yourself, contribute to the experience, and help make the game enjoyable for everyone.
