Travellers with the appropriate skills and equipment can fabricate many of the items they would otherwise need to purchase. Crafting uses an item's existing Tech Level and Cost to determine the difficulty and materials required, keeping the process simple and consistent.

---

## How Crafting Works

### Step 1: Choose an Item

Determine the item you want to fabricate and find its normal **Cost** and **Tech Level**.

---

### Step 2: Determine Complexity

The item's Tech Level determines its base **Complexity**.

| Tech Level | Complexity | Difficulty           |
| ---------- | ---------: | -------------------- |
| TL0–3      |          0 | Easy (4+)            |
| TL4–5      |          1 | Routine (6+)         |
| TL6–7      |          2 | Average (8+)         |
| TL8–9      |          3 | Difficult (10+)      |
| TL10–11    |          4 | Very Difficult (12+) |
| TL12–13    |          5 | Formidable (14+)     |
| TL14–15    |          6 | Formidable (16+)     |
| TL16+      |          7 | Impossible (18+)     |
The GM may adjust an item's Complexity when its actual construction is substantially simpler or more complicated than its Tech Level would normally suggest.

---

### Step 3: Gather Materials

Fabricating an item requires materials worth **50% of the item's normal purchase price**.

**Materials Cost = Item Cost × 0.5**

For example, an item costing **Cr10,000** requires **Cr5,000** worth of materials.

Materials may be purchased, salvaged, recovered from other equipment, or obtained through other means at the GM's discretion.

---

### Step 4: Check the Tech Level

The equipment and facilities being used must be capable of working with the item's Tech Level.

A Traveller cannot normally fabricate an item using equipment that is insufficient for its technological complexity.

The GM may allow exceptions when unusual equipment, expertise, or circumstances make fabrication possible.

---

### Step 5: Make the Crafting Check

Make a standard Traveller skill check using the appropriate skill for the item.

**2D6 + INT or EDU Modifier + Skill**

The Difficulty is determined by the item's Complexity.

The appropriate skill depends on what is being fabricated.

| Skill | Typical Applications |
|---|---|
| **Mechanic** | Weapons, armour, tools, vehicles, machinery, mechanical components |
| **Electronics** | Computers, communications, sensors, drones, electronic equipment |
| **Engineer** | Starship systems, drives, power systems, life support, complex engineering systems |
| **Science** | Chemicals, drugs, biological materials, scientific equipment |
| **Medic** | Medical equipment, prosthetics, medical supplies, and treatments |
| **Profession** | Specialised trades such as cooking, tailoring, and other crafts not covered by another skill |

---

### Step 6: Complete the Item

If the check succeeds, the Traveller successfully fabricates the item.

If the check fails, the item is not completed. The GM determines whether the materials, time, or both are lost based on the circumstances.

Particularly successful or unsuccessful attempts may produce additional results at the GM's discretion.

---

## Example

A Traveller wants to fabricate a **Laser Rifle**.

**Cost:** Cr6,000  
**Tech Level:** TL10

A TL10 item has **Complexity 4**, making the check **Very Difficult (12+)**.

The Traveller must provide materials worth:

**Cr6,000 × 0.5 = Cr3,000**

The Traveller has **Mechanic 2**, **EDU +1**, and chooses EDU for the check.

They roll:

**2D6 + 3 vs. Difficult (12+)**

On a successful check, the Laser Rifle is fabricated.

---

> [!tip] In Short
> **1. Choose the item.**
>
> **2. Check its Tech Level and determine Complexity.**
>
> **3. Provide materials worth 50% of its Cost.**
>
> **4. Make an INT or EDU + Skill check.**
>
> **5. Meet the Difficulty and the item is successfully fabricated.**

## Inventing Items

Any Traveller with a relevant crafting skill can invent a new item.

### Step 1: Describe the Concept

Tell the GM what the item does. The GM then sets a **Benchmark**, which gives the item a Cost and Tech Level:

- **Weapons:** design the weapon on the **MFC Advanced Weapon Design Worksheet**. The worksheet's final Cost and Tech Level are the Benchmark.
- **Armour:** start from a base armour in the Armor catalogue (or the CSC) and add any CSC armour modifications. The combined Cost and the highest Tech Level are the Benchmark.
- **Everything else:** the GM picks the closest existing item. If the concept combines two items, add their costs together and use the higher Tech Level. If there is nothing close, the GM sets the Tech Level and a Cost at least **double** the nearest comparable item, since new ideas are never cheap.

### Step 2: Determine Complexity and Difficulty

Find the Benchmark's Complexity on the crafting table, then add **+1**. Inventing is harder than copying. Use the Difficulty for the new Complexity.

Complexity 8 (a TL16+ invention) is **Impossible (18+)** with an additional DM-2.

### Step 3: Check Facilities (Prototypes)

The workshop's Tech Level normally has to match the item's. An inventor can build up to **two Tech Levels above** their facilities using the **Prototype** rules:

|Item TL vs Workshop TL|Result|Cost|Mass|DM to Design Check and to Use|Quirks|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|Equal or lower|Standard|×1|×1|—|—|
|+1|Prototype|×5|×1|-1|1 or more|
|+2|Early Prototype|×10|×2|-2|2 or more, and Unreliable|
|+3 or more|Not possible|—|—|—|—|

Computers and electronics multiply cost and mass by **×10** (Prototype) or **×100** (Early Prototype) instead.

### Step 4: Pay for Research and Development

R&D materials cost **100% of the item's Cost**, after any Prototype multiplier. Prototypes burn through parts. As with crafting, materials can be bought, salvaged or scavenged at the GM's discretion.

### Step 5: Spend the Time

Inventing takes **1 week per point of Complexity** (minimum 1 day), working in a suitable workshop. A dedicated research facility in the colony may grant **DM+1** at the GM's discretion.

### Step 6: Make the Design Check

Roll **2D + INT modifier + the relevant skill** (as listed under Crafting Step 5) against the Difficulty from Step 2, applying any Prototype DM.

|Effect|Result|
|---|---|
|**6+**|**Breakthrough.** The design works. Roll once on the Breakthrough table.|
|**0 to 5**|**Working Blueprint.** The design works as intended.|
|**-1 to -3**|**Flawed Blueprint.** The design works but gains **1 Quirk**.|
|**-4 to -5**|**Badly Flawed Blueprint.** The design works but gains **2 Quirks**.|
|**-6 or worse**|**Failure.** The design doesn't work and half the R&D materials are lost. The GM may rule a workshop accident happens.|

Quirks from a flawed design are added on top of any Quirks a Prototype already has.

### Step 7: The Blueprint

A successful design produces **one working item** (the prototype unit) and a **Blueprint**.

- **Copies** are made using the normal **Crafting** rules (materials 50% of Cost) at the item's normal Complexity, not the +1 used for inventing. The inventor gains **DM+1** when crafting their own design.
- **Quirks are part of the Blueprint**, so every copy has them until the design is refined.
- **Prototype Blueprints** stay Prototypes (with their cost, mass, DM and Quirks) until the copies are made in a workshop of the item's full Tech Level.
- **Blueprints are information.** They can be copied, traded, sold or stolen.

### Refining a Blueprint

A Traveller can work on a flawed Blueprint to remove its Quirks. This takes the same time as the original design, costs **50% of the item's Cost**, and uses the same Design Check. Success removes **one Quirk**, or **two** with Effect 6+. A failure with Effect -6 or worse adds a Quirk.

Upgrading the workshop to the item's full Tech Level removes the Prototype penalties, but only refining removes Quirks.

|1D|Breakthrough|
|---|---|
|1|**Efficient:** copies cost 25% less in materials.|
|2|**Lightweight:** the item's mass is reduced by 25%.|
|3|**Intuitive:** DM+1 to all checks made using the item.|
|4|**Robust:** the item can never become Unreliable, and it gains DM+2 on Malfunction table rolls.|
|5|**Elegant:** copies are crafted at one Complexity lower.|
|6|**Signature Feature:** the design includes one free Upgrade (see below), which doesn't count against the Upgrade limit.|

## Upgrading Items

Any Traveller with the relevant crafting skill can improve an existing item.

- **Difficulty:** the item's normal crafting Difficulty (from its Complexity).
- **Cost:** **25% of the item's Cost** per Upgrade, or the listed price for a CSC or MFC modification if that is higher.
- **Time:** 1 day per point of Complexity.
- **Limit:** an item can hold **3 Upgrades**. Each Upgrade already installed gives **DM-1** to install the next one.
- **Failure:** the Upgrade isn't installed and the materials are lost. With Effect -6 or worse, the item is damaged and needs repair.

### Upgrade Options

**Weapons:** any MFC receiver feature or accessory that fits the weapon:

- Accurised (DM+1 to aimed shots beyond 25m)
- High Capacity (magazine +20%)
- Lightweight (mass -20%)
- Quickdraw (Quickdraw +2)
- Recoil Compensation
- Rugged (DM+2 on Malfunction table rolls)
- Basic Stealth
- adding a scope, laser pointer or Integrated Sighting System

**Armour:** any CSC armour modification that fits the armour, or:

- **Reinforced:** Protection +1 (can be taken twice)
- **Lightened:** mass -25%

**Other items:**

- **Lightweight:** mass -25%
- **Ruggedised:** DM+2 against damage and malfunction
- **Extended Power:** double battery life or fuel capacity
- **Tuned:** DM+1 to checks using the item for one specific task

## Examples

**A standard invention.** Kael has Mechanic 2 and INT +1. He wants a suppressed bolt-action hunting rifle, so he designs it on the MFC worksheet. It comes out at **Cr800, TL6**. TL6 is Complexity 2, +1 for inventing, which makes Complexity 3: **Difficult (10+)**. The colony workshop is TL8, so there are no prototype penalties. R&D costs **Cr800** and takes **3 weeks**.

Kael rolls 2D + 3 and gets 11, Effect 1: a **Working Blueprint**. Copies are then crafted at Complexity 2, **Average (8+)**, for Cr400 in materials each, with Kael gaining DM+1.

**Reaching beyond the workshop.** Mira has Electronics 3 and INT +2. She wants to invent a laser rifle in the colony's TL9 workshop. The benchmark is the Laser Rifle (**Cr6,000, TL10**), one TL above the workshop, so it is a **Prototype**: cost ×5 makes R&D **Cr30,000**, she takes DM-1, and the design gets at least 1 Quirk. TL10 is Complexity 4, +1 for inventing, which makes Complexity 5: **Formidable (14+)**. It will take **5 weeks**.

Mira rolls 2D + 5 - 1 and gets 13, Effect -1: a **Flawed Blueprint**. The design picks up a second Quirk on top of the prototype's one. It works, but the colony will want to refine it before anyone trusts it in a raid.
