Travellers with the appropriate skills and equipment can fabricate many of the items they would otherwise need to purchase. Crafting uses an item's existing Tech Level and Cost to determine the difficulty and materials required, keeping the process simple and consistent.

---

## How Crafting Works

### Step 1: Choose an Item

Determine the item you want to fabricate and find its normal **Cost** and **Tech Level**.

---

### Step 2: Determine Complexity

The item's Tech Level determines its base **Complexity**.

| Tech Level | Complexity | Difficulty |
|---|---:|---|
| TL0–3 | 0 | Simple (4+) |
| TL4–5 | 1 | Easy (6+) |
| TL6–7 | 2 | Routine (8+) |
| TL8–9 | 3 | Average (10+) |
| TL10–11 | 4 | Difficult (12+) |
| TL12–13 | 5 | Very Difficult (14+) |
| TL14–15 | 6 | Formidable (16+) |
| TL16+ | 7 | Impossible (18+) |

The GM may adjust an item's Complexity when its actual construction is substantially simpler or more complicated than its Tech Level would normally suggest.

---

### Step 3: Gather Materials

Fabricating an item requires materials worth **50% of the item's normal purchase price**.

**Materials Cost = Item Cost × 0.5**

For example, an item costing **Cr10,000** requires **Cr5,000** worth of materials.

Materials may be purchased, salvaged, recovered from other equipment, or obtained through other means at the GM's discretion.

---

### Step 4: Check the Tech Level

The equipment and facilities being used must be capable of working with the item's Tech Level.

A Traveller cannot normally fabricate an item using equipment that is insufficient for its technological complexity.

The GM may allow exceptions when unusual equipment, expertise, or circumstances make fabrication possible.

---

### Step 5: Make the Crafting Check

Make a standard Traveller skill check using the appropriate skill for the item.

**2D6 + INT or EDU Modifier + Skill**

The Difficulty is determined by the item's Complexity.

The appropriate skill depends on what is being fabricated.

| Skill | Typical Applications |
|---|---|
| **Mechanic** | Weapons, armour, tools, vehicles, machinery, mechanical components |
| **Electronics** | Computers, communications, sensors, drones, electronic equipment |
| **Engineer** | Starship systems, drives, power systems, life support, complex engineering systems |
| **Science** | Chemicals, drugs, biological materials, scientific equipment |
| **Medic** | Medical equipment, prosthetics, medical supplies, and treatments |
| **Profession** | Specialised trades such as cooking, tailoring, and other crafts not covered by another skill |

---

### Step 6: Complete the Item

If the check succeeds, the Traveller successfully fabricates the item.

If the check fails, the item is not completed. The GM determines whether the materials, time, or both are lost based on the circumstances.

Particularly successful or unsuccessful attempts may produce additional results at the GM's discretion.

---

## Example

A Traveller wants to fabricate a **Laser Rifle**.

**Cost:** Cr6,000  
**Tech Level:** TL10

A TL10 item has **Complexity 4**, making the check **Difficult (12+)**.

The Traveller must provide materials worth:

**Cr6,000 × 0.5 = Cr3,000**

The Traveller has **Mechanic 2**, **EDU +1**, and chooses EDU for the check.

They roll:

**2D6 + 3 vs. Difficult (12+)**

On a successful check, the Laser Rifle is fabricated.

---

> [!tip] In Short
> **1. Choose the item.**
>
> **2. Check its Tech Level and determine Complexity.**
>
> **3. Provide materials worth 50% of its Cost.**
>
> **4. Make an INT or EDU + Skill check.**
>
> **5. Meet the Difficulty and the item is successfully fabricated.**
