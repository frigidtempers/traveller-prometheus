Creating a Traveller character in _Prometheus_ is a process of determining who your character is, where they came from, and what they did before the campaign began. Follow the steps below in order.

> [!warning] Character Generation  
> Traveller character creation is a living process. Your character's career may not go according to plan. **Survival and Advancement rolls must be made whenever required.** Failed rolls can have lasting consequences, including injury, career changes, or leaving a career unexpectedly.

---

## Step 1. Select or Roll Your Xenotype and Planetary Origin

Begin by determining your character's biological heritage and upbringing.

### Xenotype

Select or randomly determine your character's **Xenotype**.

Your Xenotype determines your character's unique biological traits, advantages, and disadvantages.

### Planetary Origin

Select or randomly determine your character's **Planetary Origin**.

Your Planetary Origin represents the world or environment in which your character was raised and provides additional traits that reflect their upbringing.

Record both on your character sheet.

---

## Step 2. Roll Characteristics

Roll your character's Characteristics using the method presented in the _Traveller Core Rulebook_.

Record your:

- **STR** (Strength)
    
- **DEX** (Dexterity)
    
- **END** (Endurance)
    
- **INT** (Intelligence)
    
- **EDU** (Education)
    
- **SOC** (Social Standing)
    
- **LUCK** (Luck)
    

Apply any modifiers granted by your Xenotype and Planetary Origin where appropriate.

---

## Step 3. Select Background Skills

Before beginning a career, determine the skills your character acquired during their early life.

Select your **Background Skills** according to the rules in the _Traveller Core Rulebook_.

Your Background Skills represent the basic knowledge and practical abilities your character developed before entering adulthood and beginning their career.

---

## Step 4. Choose Your Path

At this point, choose how your character spent their early adult years.

You have two options:

### Pre-Career Education

Choose to continue your education before entering the workforce.

If you choose **Pre-Career Education**, resolve your education package according to its rules before beginning your first Career Term.

After completing your Pre-Career Education, proceed to **Career Term 1**.

### Enter the Workforce

You may instead enter the workforce immediately and attempt your first Career Term.

If you choose this option, proceed directly to **Career Term 1**.

> [!info] Career Terms  
> Pre-Career Education does not replace a Career Term. A character who chooses Pre-Career Education will complete their education and then proceed through three Career Terms.

---

# Career Terms

Each Career Term represents a period of your character's adult life spent pursuing a particular career.

For each Career Term:

1. Select a Career.
    
2. Determine your assignment or specialization.
    
3. Make your **Survival Roll**.
    
4. If you survive the term, make your **Advancement Roll**.
    
5. Resolve the results of your term.
    
6. Roll for any events, mishaps, or Life Events required by your career.
    
7. Record your skills, benefits, rank, and other changes.
    
8. Determine whether you continue in the career or leave it.
    

> [!warning] Survival and Advancement  
> **Do not skip your Survival or Advancement Rolls.**
> 
> These rolls are an important part of character generation. A character's history is shaped not only by what they choose to do, but also by whether they succeed or fail along the way.

---

## Step 5. Career Term 1

### If You Chose Pre-Career Education

After completing Pre-Career Education, enter your first Career Term.

**Make your Survival and Advancement Rolls as required.**

### If You Entered the Workforce

Your first Career Term begins immediately after Step 4.

**Make your Survival and Advancement Rolls as required.**

---

## Step 6. Career Term 2

Enter your second Career Term.

You may continue in your previous career or enter a new one, subject to the normal career rules.

**Make your Survival and Advancement Rolls as required.**

Resolve all events, skills, benefits, and other results from the term.

---

## Step 7. Career Term 3

Enter your third and final Career Term.

You may continue your previous career or enter a new one, subject to the normal career rules.

**Make your Survival and Advancement Rolls as required.**

Resolve all events, skills, benefits, and other results from the term.

### Pre-Career Education Characters

Characters who chose Pre-Career Education still complete three Career Terms.

Their progression is:

**Pre-Career Education → Career Term 1 → Career Term 2 → Career Term 3**

Characters who entered the workforce immediately progress through:

**Career Term 1 → Career Term 2 → Career Term 3**

---

# Finalize Your Character

Once all three Career Terms have been completed:

- Record your final Characteristics.
    
- Record all Skills and their levels.
    
- Record your final Career and Rank.
    
- Record all Life Events.
    
- Record Allies, Contacts, Rivals, and Enemies.
    
- Determine your Benefits and mustering-out results.
    
- Record your starting Equipment and Credits.
    
- Record your Standard of Living.
    
- Record any Xenotype traits and Planetary Origin traits.
    
- Record any injuries, scars, disabilities, or other consequences gained during character creation.
    

Your character is now ready for Prometheus.
