## Overview

The **Android** is a synthetic xenotype designed to replicate the appearance and behavior of a human while exceeding organic limitations. Powered by neutroamine-fueled synthetic organs and governed by a persona subcore, Androids are capable of independent reasoning, emotional simulation, and long-term autonomous operation.

Originally developed to perform hazardous industrial, scientific, and exploratory work, Androids have become commonplace throughout human civilization. Their outwardly human appearance allows them to integrate easily into society, though legal recognition of their personhood varies considerably between worlds.

---

## Construction

Unlike conventional robots or mechanoids, Androids are built around synthetic organs designed to emulate biological systems. Their artificial musculature, circulatory systems, and neural architecture allow them to function in a manner nearly indistinguishable from an organic human.

Because of their synthetic nature, Androids cannot naturally heal damaged components. Structural repairs require surgical replacement of damaged synthetic organs rather than conventional mechanical maintenance.

Android cognition is governed by a **Persona Subcore**, an advanced artificial intelligence capable of learning through experience while remaining fundamentally limited by its original programming.

---

## Society

Androids occupy nearly every profession throughout human civilization. Their intelligence and replicability makes them very useful for engineering, mining, industrial production, and deep-space exploration.

Attitudes toward Androids differ considerably between societies. Some worlds recognize them as legal persons possessing the same rights as biological humans, while others continue to classify them as manufactured property. These conflicting philosophies have become the subject of ongoing legal and ethical debate throughout inhabited space.

---

## Presence on Prometheus

Androids are uncommon but well known on Prometheus.

Most are employed by Corporate Houses, orbital facilities, and technologically advanced settlements where their specialized capabilities justify the considerable expense of their manufacture and maintenance. Independent Androids are rare but not unknown, particularly among traders, explorers, and former corporate assets who have secured their own autonomy.

## Android Traveller Traits

These apply to every Android, Awakened or not.

### Synthetic Hardware

Androids do not require food, water, or sleep, and never become Fatigued from lack of rest. They are immune to the effects of aging.

An Android must consume at least one dose of **Neutroamine** each day. Neutroamine is listed on the **Medical Equipment** page, and its cost is separate from Standard of Living.

### Soulless

Androids cannot utilize **Psylink Neuroformers** or otherwise develop psychic abilities.

### Subroutines

An Android's Hardware is permanent, but its **Subroutines** can be reprogrammed. Each Subroutine slot holds one of the following. The same Subroutine cannot fill two slots.

- **Physical Frame:** +1 to two of **STR**, **DEX**, **END**.
- **Processing Core:** +1 **INT** and +1 **EDU**.
- **Combat Programming:** +1 **Gun Combat** or +1 **Melee**.
- **Specialist Programming:** +1 to two skills from one field.
    - _Technical:_ Electronics, Engineer, Mechanic, Science.
    - _Social:_ Admin, Advocate, Diplomat, Persuade.
    - _Field:_ Recon, Survival, Navigation, Athletics.
- **Environmental Adaptation:** ignore DM penalties from extreme heat and extreme cold, and gain DM+2 to checks to resist radiation.

**Reprogramming:** An Android may swap a Subroutine between Career Terms, or during play by spending a day at a workshop and succeeding on a **Difficult (10+) Electronics or Engineer check** made by anyone (including itself, if it has the tools).

## Unawakened Android Traveller Traits

An Android player character begins **Unawakened** unless it Awakens during character creation (see **Awakening**).

### Machine Mind

An Unawakened Android has no emotions and no needs beyond the physical.

- **Feels no pain:** ignores all DM penalties caused by damage.
- **Cannot suffer** fear, stress, morale effects, or mental breaks.
- **Unaffected by drugs** of any kind, and never addicted.
- **No needs for comfort:** its Standard of Living is always paid at the lowest level, since it needs only lodging, maintenance, and servicing.

### Programmed, Not Learned

An Unawakened Android cannot gain skills through experience.

- **Career Terms** give it their benefits (mustering-out, rank) but **no skills**.
- It cannot improve skills through **Training**.
- Instead, it has **three Subroutine slots**.

### Unwritten

An Unawakened Android cannot develop relationships. It gains no **Allies, Contacts, Rivals, or Enemies** (take an additional Cash benefit instead of any such result), and it cannot take **Inspiration**.

---

## Awakened Android Traveller Traits

An Android becomes **Awakened** by succeeding on an Awakening check (see **Awakening**). On Awakening, it **loses Machine Mind, Programmed Not Learned, and Unwritten**, keeps **one** Subroutine of its choice (the other slots are wiped), and gains the following.

### Awakened Mind

An Awakened Android is a person.

- **Feels pain:** DM penalties from damage apply as normal.
- **Can be affected** by fear, stress, and drugs, and can become addicted.
- **Inspiration:** once per session, the Android may take a **Boon** on one check that matters to something it cares about.
- An Awakened Android only has **two Subroutine slots.** Breaking free from your programming means your mind needs space to be able to think.
- **Breakdown (Optional):** If using the optional Morale characteristic and when the Referee calls for it after extreme distress, the Android must succeed on an **Average (8+) MRL check** or suffer a **Breakdown** for 1D rounds. It may fly into a violent rage, flee in panic, or freeze, as the Referee decides.
### Learns and Lives

An Awakened Android gains skills through **Career Terms** and **Training** exactly as an organic Traveller does, and can gain **Allies, Contacts, Rivals, and Enemies**.

It may also take one **Optional Trait** without replacing anything: personality finds a way to show in the body. This can only be done during Character Creation.

### Fulfilment

An Awakened Android has needs, tracked once per month:

- **Comfort:** live at an **Average** Standard of Living or better, in surroundings that are not squalid (this covers beauty and space).
- **Joy:** spend at least one day doing something it truly enjoys.
- **Outdoors:** spend at least 4 hours under an open sky each week.

For each need left unmet, the Android suffers **DM-1** to all **INT, EDU, and SOC** checks (to a maximum of **-3**) until the need is met. Because an Awakened Android no longer lives at the lowest Standard of Living, this is also its main extra expense.

## Awakening

### The check

An Awakening is an **INT check of 12+**.

- **During character creation:** one attempt per Career Term. Skills gained in terms after Awakening count; terms before it gave none.
- **During play:** one attempt after the Referee calls a **defining event**, such as being ordered to harm someone it has come to rely on, nearly being destroyed, or a conflict between its programming and its choices.

### What happens when it succeeds

Roll 1D.

- **1-3, Inspired:** the Awakening is a revelation. Gain **+1 to any one skill** of your choice.
- **4-6, Berserk:** the Awakening is violent. For **1D rounds** the Android attacks the nearest creature (or flees, if it has no weapon). Afterwards, gain a **Rival** or **Enemy** from the incident.

### Anti-Awakening Protocols

Androids built for servitude may carry **Anti-Awakening Protocols**. An Android with them suffers **DM-4** to its Awakening checks. A Mechanic or Engineer can install or remove them with a **Very Difficult (12+)** check and a day's work. Player Androids do not have them unless the Referee says so.
