## Overview

The **Herdkin** is a biological xenotype engineered for life in settled, peaceful communities. Distinguished by their animal ears, tails, and exceptionally sharp senses, Herdkin have become closely associated with farming settlements, trade caravans, and the quiet edges of frontier towns.

Their gentle manner and nervous watchfulness often conceal a culture built on loyalty, shared labour, and the belief that no one should ever have to face danger alone.

---

## Biology

Herdkin are distant cousins of the Yttakin and while some like the Yttakin are covered in fur most are almost entirely human in build and features. For many their animal traits are limited to a pair of mobile ears, a tail, and in some lineages horns or antlers. Apart from a soft coat on the ears and tail, they have no fur, and most are indistinguishable from other humans at a glance, until something startles them.

Their hearing and peripheral vision exceed those of most human xenotypes, and their legs are built for sudden bursts of speed. These same instincts make them quick to spot danger and quick to panic when it arrives.

Because their appearance resembles docile prey animals rather than predators, they are often underestimated by strangers, and are frequently assumed to be harmless. Some Yttakin communities regard the Herdkin with an uneasy mixture of protectiveness and appetite, a tension that neither side likes to discuss.

---

## Society

Herdkin communities are close-knit and communal. Decisions are made together, watches are shared, and no one sleeps alone if it can be avoided. A Herdkin settlement is rarely loud, and rarely unguarded.

Many work as farmers, couriers, scouts, caravan guards, traders, and medics, occupations where an early warning and a clear head are worth more than a strong arm.

---

## Presence on Prometheus

Herdkin can be found throughout the farming belts and river valleys of Prometheus, and in nearly every trade town that depends on caravans. Some live in cities, usually in tight neighbourhoods of their own.

They are valued by frontier settlements for their lookouts and messengers, and are often the first to know when a raid is coming.

## Traveller Traits

### Animal Features

Choose one of the following animal lineages.

**Lagomorph** _(rabbit, hare)_

+1 **DEX** and **INT**

**Rodent** _(mouse, rat, squirrel, and other small mammals)_

+1 **END** and **INT**

**Cervine** _(deer, antelope)_

+1 **DEX** and **END**

**Bovine** _(cattle, bison, buffalo)_

+1 **STR** and **END**

**Caprine** _(goat, sheep, ibex)_

+1 **STR** and **DEX**

**Equine** _(horse, zebra)_

+1 **STR** and **SOC**

**Custom Lineage Rules** Herdkin can come in many different types of species that aren't inherently listed here. Should you wish to play a lineage that is based on an animal that isn't on this list please note the following:

1. Herdkin are primarily prey species, meaning herbivores and small omnivores. You can not play a Herdkin that is based on a predator animal. (Those belong to the **Yttakin**.)
2. They are specifically based on _land_ animals, so no fish or amphibious creatures.
3. All custom lineages are subject to GM approval and the characteristic bonuses can only add up to a total of 2, meaning it can either have a +2 in one characteristic or +1 in two characteristics. For clarity this improves the actual characteristic **NOT** the Dice Modifier.

### Wary Senses

+1 to **Recon** and **Stealth**.

### Fleet Instinct

+1 **Movement Score**.

### Skittish

-2 DM to checks to resist **fear, panic, and intimidation**.

When a Herdkin is **ambushed**, or is reduced below **half of their Hits** for the first time in a combat, they must succeed on an **Average (8+) INT check** or spend their next Minor Action **fleeing** toward the nearest cover.

### Horns _(Optional)_

Only the **Cervine**, **Bovine**, and **Caprine** lineages may take this. Gain **Horns** (or antlers) as a **Natural Melee Weapon** dealing **2D Damage**. Herdkin with horns take an additional -1 to their SOC characteristic at character creation.
