## Overview

Undines are humans spliced with the genes of water life, created to settle worlds where dry land is scarce, drowned, or poisoned. Their gene-lines draw on fish, whales and porpoises, octopuses and squid, and crabs and shrimp, so two Undines can look nothing alike. Some have gills and scales, some have smooth grey skin and a blowhole-like nostril at the back of the neck, and some have shell plating across their shoulders.

What they share is a need for water. Every Undine is at home in it, and every Undine suffers without it.

## Biology

Most Undines breathe through both lungs and gills, switching between them without thinking. The cetacean lines are the exception: they have no gills, but their lungs and blood hold enough oxygen to stay under for half an hour or more.

Each family senses the water in its own way. Fish-lines feel movement through a **lateral line** of pressure-sensitive pores running from jaw to hip. Whale and porpoise lines **echolocate**, clicking and listening to the echoes. Cephalopod lines can **change the colour and texture** of their skin, and crustacean lines grow a hard **carapace** over their shoulders, back, and forearms.

The cost is dryness. Undine skin, gills, and blowholes must be kept wet. A day or two away from water leaves them cracked, sore, and short of breath; longer than that and they begin to fail.

## Society

Undine communities are built along coasts and riverbanks, on floating platforms, and in sealed habitats on lake and sea floors. Wealth is counted in boats, nets, and dive rights. Families of mixed lineage are common, so a shark-line mother may raise an octopus-line son.

Many Undines distrust "drylanders" who see water only as something to cross, and drylanders in turn often treat Undines as useful hired hands for anything wet and dangerous. Off-world, Undines find steady work as divers, salvagers, hydroponics crew, and sailors, and are known to pay extra for quarters with a tank or a proper bath.

## Presence on Prometheus

Small Undine fishing towns cling to the coasts, lakes, and islands, trading fish and salvage for goods from inland settlements. A few sealed underwater habitats are rumored to exist, though few drylanders have seen one.

## Undine Traveller Traits

### Aquatic

Undines swim at their full Movement, and ignore the DM penalties for acting underwater. They can safely dive to **100 metres** without equipment. The **Abyssal** and **Whale** lineages can safely dive to **1,000 metres**.

### Child of the Water

Gain **+1 Seafarer** (any specialty) or **+1 Athletics**.

### Desiccation

An Undine must spend at least **1 hour fully submerged** in water each day. Salt or fresh water both work; a full bathtub is enough.

For each day without it, the Undine takes a cumulative **DM-1** to all checks (to a maximum of **DM-3**). After the third day, it also loses **1 END** per further day. One full hour of immersion removes all of these penalties, and lost END recovers as normal.

### Aquatic Lineage

Choose one lineage. Your lineage gives your characteristic bonuses, and its **family** gives your breathing and family trait (described after the list).

**Fish**

- **Selachian** (shark): +1 **STR** and +1 **END**.
- **Batoid** (ray, skate): +1 **DEX** and +1 **INT**.
- **Anguilline** (eel, lamprey): +1 **DEX** and +1 **END**.
- **Reef** (reef fish, seahorse, pufferfish): +1 **DEX** and +1 **SOC**.
- **Riverine** (catfish, pike, piranha, carp): +1 **STR** and +1 **DEX**.
- **Abyssal** (anglerfish, deep-sea fish): +1 **INT** and +1 **END**.

**Cetacean**

- **Whale** (sperm whale, humpback, beluga): +1 **STR** and +1 **END**.
- **Porpoise** (porpoise, dolphin, orca): +1 **DEX** and +1 **INT**.

**Cephalopod**

- **Octopod** (octopus): +1 **DEX** and +1 **INT**.
- **Teuthid** (squid, cuttlefish): +1 **STR** and +1 **INT**.

**Crustacean**

- **Decapod** (crab, lobster, crayfish): +1 **STR** and +1 **END**.
- **Stomatopod** (mantis shrimp, shrimp): +1 **STR** and +1 **DEX**.

> **Custom Lineage Rules**
> 
> 1. A custom lineage must be based on an animal that **lives in water**, fresh or salt. Choose the family it is closest to (fish, cetacean, cephalopod, or crustacean); it uses that family's breathing and family trait.
> 2. Animals that only partly live in water, such as seals, otters, sea turtles, and amphibians, need Referee approval. Land animals belong to the Yttakin or Herdkin.
> 3. The bonuses must total **2**, and each improves the actual characteristic, not its DM.

### Family Traits

**Fish: Gills and Lateral Line**

Fish-lines breathe both air and water. While submerged, they sense every moving creature within **20 metres**, even in total darkness, and cannot be surprised by them.

**Cetacean: Air-Breather and Echolocation**

Cetaceans **cannot breathe water**. They can hold their breath for **30 minutes** (**Whale:** 90 minutes) before they begin to suffocate.

While submerged, they sense every creature and object within **50 metres**, moving or still, even in total darkness, and cannot be surprised. Out of water, echolocation works only within **5 metres**.

**Cephalopod: Gills and Chromatophores**

Cephalopod-lines breathe both air and water. They can change the colour and texture of their skin at will, gaining **DM+2** to **Stealth** checks while wearing little or no clothing. Their boneless joints also give **DM+2** to escape grapples and restraints.

**Crustacean: Gills and Carapace**

Crustacean-lines breathe both air and water. Their carapace gives **Armour +1**, which stacks with worn armour.

### Natural Weapon (Optional)

An Undine may take its lineage's natural weapon at the cost of **-1 SOC**.

- **Selachian, Jaws:** a bite attack that deals **2D** damage.
- **Batoid, Barbed Tail:** a tail-stinger attack that deals **2D** damage.
- **Anguilline, Electric Organ:** a touch attack that deals **2D** damage and has the **Stun** trait. Underwater, it also affects every other creature within 2 metres.
- **Reef, Venomous Spines:** anyone who grapples the Undine or hits it with an unarmed attack takes **1D** damage.
- **Riverine, Barbels:** whisker-like taste organs. Within **5 metres**, the Undine ignores DM penalties from darkness, murky water, and smoke.
- **Abyssal, Lure:** a bioluminescent organ that lights a **5 metre** radius at will. The Undine ignores DM penalties from dim light.
- **Whale, Fluke Slam:** a tail strike that deals **2D** damage. Underwater, a target hit is pushed **2 metres** away.
- **Porpoise, Teeth:** a bite attack that deals **2D** damage.
- **Octopod, Ink Sac:** once per hour, release a cloud of ink that fills a **3 metre** radius underwater, blocking sight through it for **1D** rounds.
- **Teuthid, Beak:** a bite attack that deals **2D** damage.
- **Decapod, Pincers:** a claw attack that deals **2D** damage. Gain **DM+1** to grapple checks.
- **Stomatopod, Club Strike:** a punch that deals **2D** damage with **AP 2**.
