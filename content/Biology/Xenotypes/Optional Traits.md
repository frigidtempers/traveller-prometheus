# Optional Traits

>[!info] Orbital Communications Console Uplink

> **Archive Status:** Active

> **Classification:** Optional Character Traits

> **Designation:** Optional Traits
> 
> **Source Reliability:** High

Optional Traits represent unusual circumstances surrounding a Traveller's **birth**, **upbringing**, **temperament**, or the **marks** their life has left on them. They are entirely optional and exist to help further customize a character beyond their Planetary Origin, Xenotype, and Career.

Unless otherwise noted, a Traveller may select **one** Optional Trait. **Birth** and **Upbringing** traits are only available at character creation. **Temperament** and **Marks** traits may also be taken later, with Referee approval, once the story supports them.

Xenotypes other than the **Baseliner** must **replace one of their Xenotype's Traveller Traits** to take a **Birth** or **Upbringing** trait, and lose that trait's benefits and drawbacks. The replaced trait must be one that grants a benefit; a pure drawback cannot be traded away. **Temperament** and **Marks** traits already pair a benefit with a drawback, so they replace nothing. Baseliners may take one Optional Trait without replacing anything (see **Diaspora**). All choices are subject to Referee approval.

**Appearance.** A Traveller's appearance is up to the player. Fur, scales, horns, ears, tails, augmented eyes, and every other cosmetic choice cost nothing and change nothing in the rules.

## Birth

### Vat-Grown

_"Born in a laboratory rather than a nursery."_

Vat-grown humans and xenotypes are cultivated entirely within artificial gestation facilities. Originally developed to accelerate colonization and replace labor shortages, vat-grown individuals now fill every role imaginable throughout the galaxy. While biologically indistinguishable from naturally born people, many spend their lives questioning whether experience can truly replace childhood.

**Effect**

Instead of selecting **Background Skills** during character creation, immediately enter a **Pre-Career Education** of your choice, subject to Referee approval.

### Cryptosleeper

_"You went to sleep in one century and woke up in another."_

Cryptosleep caskets are still found in forgotten vaults and wrecked ships across the Rim. Those who survive the waking often carry knowledge nobody alive remembers how to use, and little idea of how the world has changed.

**Effect**

- Gain **+1** to one of **Electronics, Engineer, Mechanic, or Science** (old-world knowledge).
- Suffer **DM-1** to **SOC** checks involving local customs and current events.

### Void-Born

_"Gravity is something that happens to other people."_

Raised aboard ships and stations, Void-Born feel more at home in a vacuum suit than on a planet's surface.

**Effect**

- Gain **+1** to **Astrogation** and **Vacc Suit**.
- Suffer **DM-1** to **Athletics** checks while on a planet's surface.

## Upbringing

### Orphan of the Rim

_"The colony raised you. Nobody remembers whose child you were."_

Left on the doorstep of a frontier settlement, some children are raised by everyone and belong to no one.

**Effect**

- Gain **two Contacts** from the community that raised you.
- Gain **+1** to **Streetwise**.
- Lose **one Benefit roll** at character creation (minimum 0).

### Devout

_"Faith is a family business."_

Raised within one of the Ideoligious Groups of Prometheus, a Devout Traveller's first friends, first rivals, and first rules all came from their faith.

**Effect**

Choose one group from **Ideoligious Groups**.

- Gain one **Ally** within that group.
- Gain **DM+1** to **Diplomat**, **Advocate**, and **Persuade** checks with members of that group.
- Suffer **DM-1** to the same checks with members of any group it opposes.

### Raised by Machines

_"Your first words were command syntax."_

Some of the Rim's most isolated facilities are run by automation, with only a handful of people to tend them.

**Effect**

- Gain **+1** to **Electronics** and **Mechanic**.
- Suffer **DM-1** to **Carouse** and **Diplomat** checks with people you do not know well.

### Heirloom

_"One thing was passed down to you, and everyone wants it."_

A well-made weapon, a rare instrument, or a family trinket with a long history.

**Effect**

- Begin play with one item worth up to **Cr5,000**, subject to Referee approval.
- Gain a **Rival** who wants it back.

## Temperament

These traits are modelled on the personality traits in RimWorld.

### Tough

_"Hit it again. It just makes it angry."_

**Effect**

- **Armour +1** (natural, stacks with worn armour).
- Suffer **-1 DEX**.

### Nimble

_"You have never been where the hit landed."_

**Effect**

- **DM+1** to **Dodge** reactions and to **Athletics (dexterity)** checks to avoid hazards.
- Suffer **-1 STR**.

### Iron-Willed

_"They tried everything."_

**Effect**

- **DM+2** to checks to resist fear, coercion, and mental breaks.
- Suffer **DM-1** to **Diplomat** and **Persuade** checks (people notice a person who will not bend).

### Kind

_"You never learned how to hurt people."_

**Effect**

- **DM+1** to **Diplomat** and **Medic** checks when dealing with people in distress.
- Suffer **DM-2** to **Persuade** checks involving intimidation.

### Bloodlust

_"It is never personal. It is always good."_

**Effect**

- **DM+1** to **Melee** attack rolls and to the first attack roll you make in any combat.
- Suffer **DM-1** to **Diplomat** and **Advocate** checks (you cannot hide the grin).

### Night Owl

_"The day is for people who need to see."_

**Effect**

- **DM+1** to all checks made between sunset and sunrise.
- Suffer **DM-1** to all checks made in full daylight.

### Jogger

_"You were running before anyone asked."_

**Effect**

- **+1 Movement Score**.
- Suffer **DM-1** to **Stealth** checks while moving.

## Marks

### Scarred

_"Everyone looks, then looks away."_

Old injuries, burns, or surgical repairs mark you out in any crowd.

**Effect**

- **DM+1** to **Persuade** checks involving intimidation.
- Suffer **DM-1** to **Carouse** and **Diplomat** checks with strangers.
