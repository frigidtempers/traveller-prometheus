Optional Traits represent unusual circumstances surrounding a Traveller's birth, upbringing, or appearance. They are entirely optional and exist to help further customize a character beyond their Planetary Origin, Xenotype, and Career.

Unless otherwise noted, a Traveller may select **one** Optional Trait during character creation with the Referee's approval.

---

## Vat-Grown

*"Born in a laboratory rather than a nursery."*

Vat-grown humans and xenotypes are cultivated entirely within artificial gestation facilities. Originally developed to accelerate colonization and replace labor shortages, vat-grown individuals now fill every role imaginable throughout the galaxy. While biologically indistinguishable from naturally born people, many spend their lives questioning whether experience can truly replace childhood.

**Effect**

Instead of selecting **Background Skills** during character creation, immediately enter a **Pre-Career Education** of your choice, subject to Referee approval.

## Exotic

*"Not every alteration serves a practical purpose."*

Advances in gene editing, cosmetic surgery, and bionics have made dramatic alterations to one's appearance commonplace throughout the galaxy. Some Travellers emulate animals, mythical creatures, fictional heroes, or entirely original designs simply because they enjoy standing out.

Exotic traits are **purely cosmetic** and provide **no mechanical benefit**.

### Choose an Appearance

#### Alien

Typically represented by black eyes, pale skin, elongated limbs, or other features inspired by classic depictions of extraterrestrials from Old Earth media, but could represent any kind of "alien" humanoid creature. (Ex. Tau from Warhammer 40k, Sectoids from XCOM, etc.)

#### Rodent

Features inspired by mice, rats, squirrels, chipmunks, mink, and other small mammals.

#### Feline

The appearance of domestic cats or larger felines such as lions, tigers, panthers, and lynxes.

#### Lagomorph

Rabbit, hare, or pika-inspired ears and facial features.

#### Lupine

Wolf- or dog-like features ranging from subtle ears and tails to extensive gene sculpting.

#### Vulpine

Inspired by one of Old Earth's many fox species, often emphasizing long ears, fluffy tails, and narrow facial features.

#### Thrumboid

Long white hair, pale skin, and a single horn modeled after the legendary thrumbo.

#### Ursine

Bear-inspired features ranging from black bears to polar bears and everything between.

#### Draconic

Decorative scales, horns, slit pupils, tails, and other fantastical features inspired by dragons.

#### Dwarvish

Short stature, broad features, and elaborate facial hair inspired by fantasy literature and games.

#### Elvish

Graceful facial structure and elongated pointed ears inspired by one of the most enduring fantasy archetypes to survive from Old Earth.

#### Cyberpunk

Aesthetic cybernetic implants, illuminated tattoos, chromed skin panels, synthetic eyes, decorative ports, and other cosmetic augmentations that emphasize a futuristic appearance without providing mechanical benefits.

>[!note] Referee Note
>This is not a complete list of all Optional Traits and is subject to being added to at any given time. Should a Player wish to give their character an Optional Trait following character creation they should be allowed to do so subject to Referee approval and as long as it doesn't require a complete restructuring of the character generation.
