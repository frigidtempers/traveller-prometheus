Prometheus uses the **Standard of Living** rules presented in the *Traveller Core Rulebook*. Unless otherwise noted, all Travellers are assumed to pay their monthly Standard of Living expenses as normal.

A Traveller's Standard of Living represents the routine costs of maintaining their lifestyle, including food, housing, utilities, clothing, hygiene, and other day-to-day necessities while residing within civilized space.

The following rules supplement the Core Rulebook by introducing additional requirements for certain xenotypes and survival situations.

---

# Specialized Dietary Requirements

While Standard of Living covers the everyday needs of most Travellers, some genetically engineered and synthetic xenotypes possess unique biological requirements that must be met separately.

## Sanguophage

_"Blood is life. Everything else is merely food."_

A Sanguophage must consume the equivalent of **one Blood Feeding every three days**. A Feeding may consist of:

- One Blood Pack, or equivalent for the Ekkimian subtype
- Fresh blood taken from a willing donor
- Fresh blood taken from an unwilling donor
- Freshly harvested blood from a suitable creature, at the Referee's discretion

Because procuring blood is illicit, a Sanguophage who buys it must pay an additional **25% of their Standard of Living** each month, **or** roll an **Average (8+) Streetwise** check to source it themselves and avoid the extra cost. An **Ally** may acquire it for them instead, but a **Contact** cannot.

Failure to feed results in the penalties described in the Sanguophage Xenotype entry.

## Waster

_"Engineered addiction is still addiction."_

Wasters must obtain **Psychite** separately. Failure to obtain it results in the penalties described in the Waster Xenotype entry.

## Androids & Mechanents

*"Machine men, with machine minds and machine hearts!"*

Androids must obtain at least one dose of **Neutroamine** each day. Failure to do so results in the Android becoming **Fatigued** until it is supplied, at the Referee's discretion.

Synthetic beings do not consume conventional food. For Androids and Mechanents, Standard of Living represents the cost of recharge facilities, maintenance supplies, lubricants, replacement components, diagnostic software, and routine servicing. An Android's **Neutroamine** is a separate cost (see above).

## Hussar & Uhlan

*“War is life multiplied by some number that no one has ever heard of.”*

Hussars require **Go-Juice** and Uhlans require **Luciferium**. Failure to obtain a dose results in the penalties described in each xenotype entry.

# Survival Situations

Standard of Living assumes a Traveller has access to the infrastructure of a settled world or starport.

When characters are isolated from civilization, Standard of Living no longer guarantees access to food, water, or other necessities.

Examples include:

- Extended wilderness expeditions
- Rimworld exploration
- Shipwrecks
- Disaster zones
- Long-duration survival scenarios
- Military operations with disrupted supply lines

During these situations, Travellers must obtain food, water, and other supplies through scavenging, hunting, trading, or by consuming items listed in the **[[Miscellaneous Consumables & Foodstuffs]]** section.

---

# Employer-Provided Living

Many organizations provide food and accommodations as part of their employment.

Examples include:

- Colonial militias
- Corporate security forces
- Merchant crews
- Scientific expeditions
- Military personnel
- Government agencies

At the Referee's discretion, a Traveller whose employer provides housing and meals may have some or all of their Standard of Living costs waived for the duration of their employment.

Specialized dietary requirements, such as Blood Packs, Psychite, Neutroamine, Go-Juice, or Luciferium, remain the Traveller's responsibility unless explicitly supplied by their employer.

>[!note] Referee Guidance
These additions are intended to reinforce the unique biology of certain xenotypes without increasing bookkeeping for the rest of the party.
>
For most campaigns, a Traveller's Standard of Living continues to cover all routine expenses. Only specialized dietary needs and situations where normal infrastructure is unavailable should require additional tracking.
