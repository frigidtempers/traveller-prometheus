While medicine and pharmaceuticals are invaluable, most Travellers spend considerably more credits on ordinary food, drink, and everyday supplies. These items rarely make the difference between winning and losing a firefight, but they often determine whether a crew can survive weeks away from civilization.

---

## Nutrient Paste

*"It tastes exactly as nutritious as it looks."*

A dense, vitamin-fortified meal produced from recycled organic matter, algae, fungal proteins, and synthetic nutrients. Nutrient paste is cheap, shelf-stable, and nearly impossible to spoil.

**Cost:** Cr5 (One Meal)
**Mass:** —

---
## Fine Meal

*"A reminder that life is worth living."*

Fresh ingredients prepared by a competent cook. A luxury on many frontier worlds.

**Cost:** Cr40
**Mass:** .5kg

---

## Lavish Meal

*"Someone cared enough to make this."*

Prepared using premium ingredients and exceptional culinary skill.

**Cost:** Cr100
**Mass:** .5kg

---

## Packaged Water

Purified drinking water.

**Cost:** Cr2
**Mass:** .5kg

---

## Emergency Water Purifier

A portable filtration system capable of rendering most natural water sources drinkable.

Provides clean water indefinitely with routine maintenance.

**Cost:** Cr150
**Mass:** 2kg

---

## Blood Pack

*"Certified pathogen-free."*

One liter of preserved human blood collected for medical use. Though intended for transfusions, blood packs are commonly purchased in secret by Sanguophages.

A Blood Pack satisfies one blood feeding.

**Cost:** Cr100
**Mass:** .5kg

---

## Hemogen Pack

Concentrated, stabilized hemogenic plasma refined specifically for Sanguophages.

Unlike standard blood, Hemogen Packs remain viable for years without refrigeration.

A Hemogen Pack counts as one blood feeding.

**Cost:** Cr200
**Mass:** .5kg

---

## Preserved Flesh

Vacuum-sealed cultured muscle tissue intended for emergency rations, xenobiological research, and carnivorous xenotypes.

Although technically edible by anyone, most people find the texture revolting.

**Cost:** Cr30
**Mass:** 2kg

---

## Nutrient Slurry

Industrial food concentrate supplied in five-liter containers.

Enough nutrition for ten meals.

Common aboard freighters and mining stations.

**Cost:** Cr35
**Mass:** 5kg

---

## Coffee

A stimulant beverage brewed from genetically engineered coffee plants descended from Old Earth cultivars.

Removes fatigue penalties for approximately one hour.

**Cost:** Cr5
**Mass:** —

---

## Tea

A variety of herbal and traditional teas consumed throughout settled space.

Provides no mechanical benefit beyond hydration and comfort.

**Cost:** Cr3
**Mass:** —

---

## Alcohol

Standard distilled spirits, beer, or wine.

See **Drugs** for intoxication rules.

**Cost:** Cr10 per serving
**Mass:** .5kg

---

## Cooking Kit

A compact field kitchen containing cookware, utensils, portable burners, and food preparation equipment.

Allows meals to be prepared while away from civilization.

**Cost:** Cr75
**Mass:** 1kg
