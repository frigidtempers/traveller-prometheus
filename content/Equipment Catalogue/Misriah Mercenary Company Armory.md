## Overview

The **Misriah Mercenary Company** is one of the best-equipped private military forces to operate on the Rim. It is organised like a regular army, with line riflemen, orbital drop troopers, special operations teams and a small cadre of augmented **Spartan** operatives in MJOLNIR powered armour. It arms them almost entirely from its own foundries.

Misriah hardware is expensive, rugged and built to a common standard. Every Misriah firearm is vacuum-rated, fires the company's own armour-piercing and high-explosive munitions, and links to the HUD in a Misriah helmet. Misriah weapons sometimes turn up on the open market as battlefield pickups, stolen shipments or surplus sold off by a company quartermaster, but the munitions are harder to find than the guns.

This catalogue also lists the **archotech-pattern** weapons and armour recovered from **the Reliquary**, the archotech site Misriah discovered and has been fighting over ever since (see below).

### Rules Used

- Every firearm on this page is built with the **Personal Weapon Design** rules from the _Mercenary Field Catalogue_ (MFC), using the calibres and munitions Misriah fields.
- Armour comes from the _Central Supply Catalogue_ (CSC).
- Where Traveller has no equivalent (energy shields, plasma weapons that vent heat, homing needles), there is a short **Special Rule**, so you can see exactly what is house-ruled.
- **MFC optional rules in use:**
    - Penetration (AP and Lo-Pen)
    - Recoil
    - Quickdraw
    - Sustained Fire and Overheating (Heat)

## Special Rules

### Energy Shields

A shield is a pool of points that soaks damage **before** armour.

- Each hit drains the shield by its **full damage**. Armour, AP and Lo-Pen do not affect damage to shields.
- When the shield reaches 0, any damage left over from that hit is applied against armour as normal.
- **Recharge:** if the wearer takes no damage for one full combat round, the shield refills to full at the end of that round.
- **Plasma** weapons deal **+1D** damage to shields.
- EMP, or a hit from an overcharged plasma pistol, drops a shield to 0 and stops it recharging until the end of the target's next turn.

### Smart-Link

Every Misriah weapon carries a **Smart-Link**, which is the MFC's **Integrated Sighting System** (TL10, Cr500, 0.4kg). When the user wears a helmet with a Smart-Link HUD (Marine BDU, ODST, SPI or MJOLNIR), the weapon gains:

- **DM+1 to attack rolls at all ranges**, even when hip-fired
- **DM+6 to Recon checks** to spot targets

Without a compatible helmet it gives nothing.

### Headshot (optional)

This rule applies to the **BR75, M395B DMR, M6C Magnum, SR99-S7, Type-51 Carbine, Type-31 Needle Rifle and Type-50 Beam Rifle**. An **aimed** attack with one of these weapons, with **Effect 6+**, against a target whose **shield is at 0**, **ignores armour** (the hit finds the visor or neck seal).

### Plasma Bolt Generator

Archotech-pattern plasma weapons are built as MFC energy weapons with one special feature (the MFC allows the Referee to add special features to designs). The bolt is superheated plasma that melts through armour, so it adds **Penetration +2**. This cancels the usual energy-weapon Penetration -1, giving a final Penetration +1.

### Heat Venting

Archotech-pattern energy weapons don't jam; they vent. Track Heat with the MFC Sustained Fire rules. When the weapon reaches its **Overheating threshold**, it vents automatically: it **cannot fire next round**, and then its Heat drops to 0. These weapons never roll on the Malfunction table for heat.

## The Reliquary

Somewhere in the deep wastes of Prometheus, Misriah survey teams broke into a buried structure of seamless, impossible alloy: the remains of an **archotech**, one of the god-like machine minds of the Rim. Whatever the archotech was, it is not dead. Its fabricators still run, turning out weapons no human foundry can match, and it still defends itself.

The Reliquary's guardians are **constructs**: living war-bodies the archotech grows and arms to its own unknowable designs. Misriah's analysts found the construct patterns, and the archotech's own weapon designs, catalogued in fragments of its archive. The company kept the archive's names: **Sangheili**, **Jiralhanae**, **Kig-Yar**, **Unggoy** and **Mgalekgolo** for the constructs, and **Type numbers** for the weapons. No one at Misriah knows what the names originally meant, and the archotech isn't saying.

Misriah holds the outer galleries and fights constant battles against the guardians for access to the fabricators. Most archotech-pattern weapons on the open market leaked out of those battles: pried from fallen constructs, smuggled out by Misriah personnel, or captured from Misriah convoys by raiders.

### Archotech-Pattern Hardware Rules

- **Not sold openly.** Listed costs are what a fence or a Misriah quartermaster might pay.
- **Beyond Rim workshops.** Archotech-pattern items are TL15. Under the **Inventing** rules (Crafting System), a workshop more than two Tech Levels below can't copy them at all; a TL13–14 workshop can only produce Prototypes or Early Prototypes.
- **Repairs:** a damaged archotech-pattern weapon can be repaired with the right skill, but the replacement parts must come from other archotech-pattern salvage.

They are TL12, and can be crafted, repaired and resupplied with ordinary parts.

### Type-25 Plasma Pistol

_The most common archotech-pattern weapon to leak out of the Reliquary. Weak on its own, but its overcharged bolt collapses an energy shield in one hit._

**Build:** small energy receiver (Light, 3D maximum), Efficient Beam Generation, handgun barrel, Plasma Bolt Generator, integral Light powerpack (0.3kg). Heat uses the handgun-receiver values.

**Overcharge (Special Rule):**

- Charging takes a **minor action**, and the shot uses 10 power.
- The shot gains **DM+2**, because the bolt tracks its target.
- It adds **10 Heat**, so the weapon always vents afterwards.
- A hit drops the target's shield to 0 and stops it recharging until the end of its next turn.
- Against vehicles, drones, powered armour or electronics, the target suffers **DM-2** for a round, and the Referee may rule that systems shut down.

### Type-25 Plasma Rifle

_A rapid-fire bolt thrower. Three rounds of sustained fire force it to vent on the fourth, and Misriah riflemen learn to count._

**Build:** medium energy receiver (Standard, 5D maximum, set to 4D), Efficient Beam Generation, fully automatic, assault barrel, stockless, Plasma Bolt Generator, integral Standard powerpack (0.4kg). Heat uses the assault-weapon values.

### Type-33 Needler

_Fires homing crystal needles that detonate together when enough of them hit the same target. Misriah medics hate it._

**Build:** assault receiver firing crystalline needles (light-handgun class, 2D), fully automatic with Increased Rate of Fire +1 (Auto 4), assault barrel, no stock, magazine 30.

- **Crystal Needles (Special Rule):** needles shatter against hard surfaces, giving Penetration -1 (Lo-Pen 2).
- **Homing (Special Rule, based on MFC Smart ammunition):** DM+2 to attack at any range, lost against targets in full cover.
- **Supercombine (Special Rule):** if one target takes **3 or more needle hits in the same round**, the needles detonate for an extra **5D, AP 10**. This damage **ignores shields**.

### Type-51 Carbine

_A semi-automatic marksman's weapon firing magnetically accelerated slugs._

**Build:** gauss weapon. Longarm receiver, standard gauss (4D, Penetration +2), semi-automatic, rifle barrel, full stock, scope, magazine 18.

### Type-31 Needle Rifle

_A precision rifle firing needles fast enough to punch through armour instead of shattering._

**Build:** longarm receiver, battle-rifle-class crystal needles (3D+3), semi-automatic, rifle barrel, full stock, scope, magazine 21. There is no Lo-Pen.

**Supercombine (Special Rule):** when a target has taken **3 needle hits within 3 consecutive rounds**, the needles detonate for **5D, AP 10**. This damage ignores shields.

### Spiker

_Rim-built copy (TL12). A crude, heavy automatic that fires superheated metal spikes, with blades along the barrel for when the shooting stops. Copied from the weapons Jiralhanae constructs carry, and now a favourite of pirate warbands._

**Build:** assault receiver, heavy-handgun-class superheated spikes, fully automatic, assault barrel, 40-round extended magazine. Fires **Incendiary** ammunition: a spike that gets through armour deals half that damage again the next round, ignoring armour.

### Mangler

_Rim-built copy (TL12). A brutal three-shot revolver with a blade slung under the barrel._

**Build:** handgun receiver, heavy-handgun-class hardened spikes (AP ammunition), repeater with 3 chambers, assault barrel.

### Type-50 Concussion Rifle

_Fires bursting plasma bolts that knock targets flat and shove light vehicles aside._

**Build:** medium energy receiver set to 4D, Efficient Beam Generation, carbine barrel, full stock, Plasma Bolt Generator, integral Standard powerpack (0.25kg). Heat uses the longarm values, so five quick shots force a vent.

**Concussive Bolt (Special Rule):** the bolt bursts with **Blast 3**. Everyone in the blast must make an Average (8+) STR check or be knocked prone and pushed 2m away. Light vehicles are pushed 5m.

### Type-52 Focus Rifle

_Projects a continuous beam that strips energy shields fast but does little against armour._

**Build:** large energy receiver set to 4D, Efficient Beam Generation, Intensified Pulse, fully automatic (a continuous beam), rifle barrel, full stock, scope, integral Standard powerpack (0.3kg). Heat uses the light-support-weapon values. Counts as **Plasma** for the shield rule, but has no AP.

### Type-50 Beam Rifle

_A long-range particle beam rifle. At 15kg it is a heavy weapon for a human._

**Build:** large energy receiver set to 6D, Efficient Beam Generation, Intensified Pulse, very long barrel, full stock, long-range scope, integral Heavy powerpack (0.1kg, 100 power, 16 shots).

- **Particle Beam** special feature: Penetration +2, for a final Penetration +2 (Armour-Piercing: AP 7, -3 damage).
- Heat uses the light-support-weapon values: about four quick shots before it vents.

**The constructs:**

- **Jiralhanae:** hulking brutes with no shields, only heavy armour plates that crack away under fire.

- **Unggoy:** small, numerous, expendable. The archotech grows them by the hundred.
- **Mgalekgolo ("Hunters"):** massive armoured constructs, always encountered in pairs, with a fuel rod cannon grown into one arm.
