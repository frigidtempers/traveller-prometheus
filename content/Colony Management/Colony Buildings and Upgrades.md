# Housing

Housing Upgrades increase the maximum population a colony can sustainably support.

A colony's initial Population Capacity is determined by the combined SOC of its founding Travellers. Housing Upgrades increase this capacity as the colony expands.

Each Housing Upgrade provides a fixed increase to Population Capacity.

---

| Building                      | TL  | Capacity | Cost      | Power Req. | Time to Construct |
| ----------------------------- | --- | -------- | --------- | ---------- | ----------------- |
| Homestead Housing             | 3   | +5       | Cr25,000  | 1          | 1 Month           |
| Residential Block             | 5   | +10      | Cr45,000  | 2          | 2 Months          |
| Modular Housing               | 7   | +20      | Cr80,000  | 4          | 2 Months          |
| High-Density Housing          | 9   | +50      | Cr175,000 | 8          | 3 Months          |
| Automated Residential Complex | 12  | +100     | Cr325,000 | 16         | 4 Months          |
| Arcology Housing              | 15  | +250     | Cr750,000 | 32         | 5 Months          |

---

> [!tip] Population Capacity
> **Initial Population Capacity = Combined SOC of Founding Travellers**
>
> **Housing Upgrades = Additional Population Capacity**
>
> A colony may construct multiple Housing Upgrades as its population grows.

# Food

Food represents the colony's ability to produce and provide sufficient food and clean water for its population.

A colony requires **1 Food for every point of Population**.

**Food Requirement = Population**

For example, a colony with a Population of 5 requires **Food 5** to sustain itself.

Food-producing buildings increase the colony's total Food production. Most food infrastructure provides Food in increments of 3.

---

| Building              | TL  | Food | Cost      | Power Req. | Time to Construct |
| --------------------- | --- | ---- | --------- | ---------- | ----------------- |
| Small Farm            | 3   | +3   | Cr25,000  | 0          | 1 Month           |
| Large Farm            | 3   | +6   | Cr45,000  | 0          | 2 Months          |
| Irrigated Farm        | 5   | +9   | Cr62,500  | 1          | 2 Months          |
| Hydroponics Facility  | 7   | +12  | Cr80,000  | 2          | 3 Months          |
| Advanced Hydroponics  | 10  | +18  | Cr115,000 | 4          | 4 Months          |
| Automated Agriculture | 12  | +24  | Cr150,000 | 8          | 5 Months          |

---

> [!tip] Food Requirement
> **1 Population = 1 Food Required**
>
> A colony with **5 Population** requires **Food 5**.
>
> Food-producing buildings provide Food in increments of **3**, allowing colonies to maintain a surplus as they grow.
>
> If a colony's Food production is lower than its Population, the colony has a food shortage.

# Power

Power represents the colony's ability to generate and distribute energy.

Power-producing buildings increase the colony's total Power production. Most power infrastructure provides Power in increments of 3.

---

| Building              | TL  | Power | Cost      | Time to Construct |
| --------------------- | --- | ----- | --------- | ----------------- |
| Small Generator       | 5   | +3    | Cr25,000  | 1 Month           |
| Generator Array       | 5   | +6    | Cr45,000  | 2 Months          |
| Solar Array           | 7   | +9    | Cr62,500  | 2 Months          |
| Wind Farm             | 7   | +12   | Cr80,000  | 3 Months          |
| Fusion Generator      | 10  | +18   | Cr115,000 | 4 Months          |
| Advanced Fusion Plant | 13  | +24   | Cr150,000 | 5 Months          |

---

> [!tip] Power
> Power represents the colony's available energy production.
>
> Power-producing infrastructure adds to the colony's total Power.
>
> Power requirements for individual facilities, if used, are listed with those facilities rather than with the power-generating infrastructure.

# Healthcare

Healthcare represents the colony's ability to provide medical treatment and support the recovery of its population.

Healthcare-producing buildings increase the colony's total Healthcare capacity.

Most healthcare infrastructure provides Healthcare in increments of 3.

---

| Building                 | TL  | Medic Skill | Power Req. | Cost      | Time to Construct |
| ------------------------ | --- | ----------- | ---------- | --------- | ----------------- |
| Medical Clinic           | 3   | 3           | 1          | Cr30,000  | 1 Month           |
| Medical Centre           | 5   | 6           | 2          | Cr54,000  | 2 Months          |
| Hospital                 | 6   | 9           | 4          | Cr75,000  | 2 Months          |
| Advanced Hospital        | 8   | 12          | 8          | Cr96,000  | 3 Months          |
| Automated Medical Centre | 10  | 18          | 16         | Cr138,000 | 4 Months          |
| Advanced Medical Complex | 12  | 24          | 32         | Cr180,000 | 5 Months          |

---

> [!tip] Healthcare
> Healthcare represents the colony's available medical capacity.
>
> Healthcare-producing infrastructure adds to the colony's total Healthcare.
>
> The GM determines the consequences of inadequate Healthcare based on the severity and circumstances of the medical problem.
