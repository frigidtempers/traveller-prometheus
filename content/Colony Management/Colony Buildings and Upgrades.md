# Housing

Housing Upgrades increase the maximum population a colony can sustainably support.

A colony's initial Population Capacity is determined by the combined SOC of its founding Travellers. Housing Upgrades increase this capacity as the colony expands.

Each Housing Upgrade provides a fixed increase to Population Capacity.

---

## Basic Housing

### Homestead Housing

> **Tech Level:** TL3  
> **Cost:** Cr25,000  
> **Population Capacity:** +5  
> **Construction Time:** 1 Month

Simple permanent housing suitable for a small number of residents.

---

### Residential Block

> **Tech Level:** TL5  
> **Cost:** Cr75,000  
> **Population Capacity:** +10  
> **Construction Time:** 2 Months

A larger residential structure designed to house multiple families or groups of workers.

---

## Advanced Housing

### Modular Housing

> **Tech Level:** TL7  
> **Cost:** Cr150,000  
> **Population Capacity:** +20  
> **Construction Time:** 2 Months

Prefabricated residential units that can be rapidly assembled and expanded as the colony grows.

---

### High-Density Housing

> **Tech Level:** TL9  
> **Cost:** Cr300,000  
> **Population Capacity:** +50  
> **Construction Time:** 3 Months

Efficient residential construction designed to accommodate large populations within a relatively small footprint.

---

## High-Tech Housing

### Automated Residential Complex

> **Tech Level:** TL12  
> **Cost:** Cr750,000  
> **Population Capacity:** +100  
> **Construction Time:** 4 Months

An advanced residential complex incorporating automated environmental systems and domestic infrastructure.

---

### Arcology Housing

> **Tech Level:** TL15  
> **Cost:** Cr2,000,000  
> **Population Capacity:** +250  
> **Construction Time:** 6 Months

A highly advanced residential structure capable of supporting a large population with minimal external infrastructure.

---

> [!tip] Population Capacity
> **Initial Population Capacity = Combined SOC of Founding Travellers**
>
> **Housing Upgrades = Additional Population Capacity**
>
> A colony may construct multiple Housing Upgrades as its population grows.

# Food

Food represents the colony's ability to produce and provide sufficient food and clean water for its population.

A colony requires **1 Food for every point of Population**.

**Food Requirement = Population**

For example, a colony with a Population of 5 requires **Food 5** to sustain itself.

Food-producing buildings increase the colony's total Food production. Most food infrastructure provides Food in increments of 3.

---

### Small Farm

---

> [!tip] Food Requirement
> **1 Population = 1 Food Required**
>
> A colony with **5 Population** requires **Food 5**.
>
> Food-producing buildings provide Food in increments of **3**, allowing colonies to maintain a surplus as they grow.
>
> If a colony's Food production is lower than its Population, the colony has a food shortage.
